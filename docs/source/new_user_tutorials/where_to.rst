=============
Where To Next
=============

Congratulations on completing Algan's new-user tutorials!
You should now have everything you need to start
making videos of your own.

Two catalogues are the fastest way to find out what else is available:

* :doc:`../galleries/mob_gallery` -- every Mob Algan ships with, with pictures.
* :doc:`../galleries/built_in_animations` -- the ready-made animations for
  drawing attention, moving along a path and transforming a whole diagram.

If you want to learn about more Algan functionality then
head on over to the :doc:`../advanced_user_tutorials/index`.
These tutorials are less structured and can be followed in any order.
In particular, check out the following useful tutorials:

* :doc:`../advanced_user_tutorials/saving_videos_and_images` -- how to change
  the quality settings of your videos
* :doc:`../advanced_user_tutorials/positioning_and_layout` -- all of the helpers
  for positioning mobs on screen
* :doc:`../advanced_user_tutorials/text_and_math` -- labels, LaTeX, per-glyph
  animation and animated numbers
* :doc:`../advanced_user_tutorials/images_and_textures` -- importing images, and
  painting images across surfaces
* :doc:`../advanced_user_tutorials/shaders_and_materials` -- how to make
  convincing, realistic mobs
* :doc:`../advanced_user_tutorials/audio_and_speech` -- adding sound and
  narration to your videos
* :doc:`../advanced_user_tutorials/multi_scene_projects` -- splitting a longer
  video into scenes you can re-render one at a time
* :doc:`../advanced_user_tutorials/performance_and_quality` -- what makes a
  render slow, and what to do about it

Two more pages worth knowing about:

* :doc:`../manim_migration_guide` -- if you also write Manim, or want to import
  its geometry.
* :doc:`../advanced_user_tutorials/renderer_limitations` -- what Algan's
  renderer does not do, which is the first place to look when a render does not
  come out the way you expected.

For the details of any individual class or method, use the
:doc:`reference manual <../reference>` and the documentation search.

If you need help with Algan, or just want to chat with the developers and fellow Algan
users, then join the discord server: <algan_discord>
